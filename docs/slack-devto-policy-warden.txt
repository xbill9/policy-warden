Policy Warden checks an AWS account against a policy written in plain English and reports every violation with evidence. It runs on Amazon Bedrock AgentCore Harness with Amazon Nova 2 Lite, my entry for the Agents, your rules hackathon.
Its rules are enforced by IAM, harness lifecycle hooks, a Bedrock Guardrail and code, and seven hostile prompts on the live agent, two of them injections hidden in resource tags, were all stopped.
